    Help

    1. Student. Type the name.
    2. Writing. Choose the stage (Plan, Draft or Final). Take or add a photo of the student's writing. Mark on the photo: draw under or around an error with your finger, pen or mouse, then tap an error code. A single tap adds a ^ for a missing word. Type the number of words so the app can count errors per 100 words. You can also type the text and mark it there instead, which lets the app check the language as well.
    3. Checklist. Press Check against criteria. Levels are suggested from your codes and the typed text. Review and adjust each criterion.
    4. Feedback. Press Generate feedback. Review and edit the text as required.
    5. Print or save. Press Print / Save as PDF, or Save to this computer. Next lesson, press Open saved work and choose the ZIP to keep going.

    Nothing is sent anywhere. Your work is kept on this computer for up to 30 days, until you press Clear all students. The saved ZIP is the safe copy you can open on any computer.
