Speaking practice: talk about yourself

The task 🎤

Talk about yourself for about 1 minute. There is no right or wrong answer. Have fun!

Useful phrases

  • Hi, my name is ... I’m from ...
  • My favourite ... is ... because ...
  • Last weekend I ...
  • I would like to visit ... because ...
  • Sorry, can you say that again?

Questions to practise

  1. What is your favourite food? Why? 🍜
  2. If you could visit any country, where would you go? ✈️
  3. What did you do last weekend?
  4. What animal do you like? Why? 🐶
  5. What makes you happy? 😊

Say your answers out loud. Try to give 2 or 3 sentences for each one.
