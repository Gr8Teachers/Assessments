Speaking practice: role play

The task

Role play: a new student joins your English class. Your teacher plays the new student. You:

  • greet the new student and say your name
  • ask and answer questions about where you are from, your family, work or study, and what you like to do
  • explain your ideas with because
  • ask your teacher to repeat or explain if you do not understand
  • finish the conversation politely

Useful phrases

  • Hi, welcome to our class. My name is ...
  • Where are you from? What do you do on the weekend?
  • I like Sydney because the people are friendly.
  • Sorry, can you say that again? What does ... mean?
  • Nice to meet you. See you in class. Bye.

Questions to practise

  1. Where are you from?
  2. How long have you been in Australia?
  3. What did you do last weekend?
  4. What do you like to do in your free time? Why?
  5. What are you going to do after this course?

Say your answers out loud. Give 2 or 3 sentences for each answer.
