    Give out:

    Students scan this code (or open the link) to get the answer sheet. If you give the parts out at different times, scan each part when the student finishes it. The parts join together for each student.

    Scan answers

    When a student finishes, a QR code appears on their screen. Press Start scanning and hold their screen up to the camera.

    Students can also take a screenshot of their QR code and send it to you. Press Upload QR pictures (you can choose several at once), drag the pictures onto this page, or copy a picture and press Ctrl + V.

    No camera? Paste the code instead

    If a QR reader app shows the student's code as text, paste it here.
